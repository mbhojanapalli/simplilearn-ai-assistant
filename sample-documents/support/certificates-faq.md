# Certificates — Frequently Asked Questions

## When will I receive my certificate?

Your course completion certificate is generated **automatically within 24–48 hours**
of meeting all completion criteria:

- Completing **100% of the course videos**.
- Passing the final assessment with a score of **60% or higher**.
- Submitting any required projects and having them approved by a mentor.

Once issued, the certificate appears in **My Profile → Certificates**, and a copy is
emailed to your registered email address.

## I completed the course but don't see my certificate

Please check the following:

1. All modules show as 100% complete on your dashboard.
2. Your final assessment score is at least 60%.
3. Any graded project has been **approved** (not just submitted).

If all three are met and 48 hours have passed, contact support from the Help Center
and include your course name and registered email.

## How do I download or share my certificate?

Go to **My Profile → Certificates**, then:

- Click **Download** to save a PDF.
- Click **Share** to post directly to LinkedIn, or copy the unique verification link.

Each certificate has a **verification URL** that employers can use to confirm its
authenticity.

## Is the certificate accredited?

Simplilearn certificates are recognized by industry and many employers. Certain
programs are co-branded with university or partner accreditation — check the specific
program page for details.

## My name is misspelled on the certificate

Update your name under **My Profile → Account Settings** and then request a
certificate re-issue from the Help Center. Re-issued certificates are typically ready
within 2 business days.
