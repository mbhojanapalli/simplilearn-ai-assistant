# Refund Policy

We want you to be confident in your enrollment. This policy explains when refunds
are available and how to request one.

## Self-paced learning

You may request a **full refund within 7 days** of purchase, **provided you have not
accessed more than 25%** of the course content and have not downloaded any
downloadable resources.

## Online Bootcamp / instructor-led programs

- **Full refund** if the cancellation request is raised **within 7 days** of
  enrollment **and before** attending more than one live class.
- **No refund** after attending two or more live classes, or after 7 days, whichever
  comes first.

## Corporate or group enrollments

Refunds for corporate and group purchases are governed by the agreement signed at
the time of purchase. Please contact your account manager.

## Non-refundable items

- Exam vouchers and third-party certification fees once issued.
- Promotional or heavily discounted purchases marked "non-refundable" at checkout.

## How to request a refund

1. Go to the **Help Center** and choose **Billing & Refunds**.
2. Select the order and choose **Request a refund**.
3. Provide the reason and submit.

Approved refunds are processed back to the **original payment method within 7–10
business days**. The exact timing depends on your bank or card issuer.

## Need help?

If your situation isn't covered above, raise a ticket from the Help Center and our
billing team will review your case individually.
