# Platform Access & Live Classes — Help

## I can't access my live class

Live classes run inside the learning portal. If you can't join, work through these
steps in order:

1. **Check the schedule.** Open **My Learning → Live Classes** and confirm the class
   start time and your time zone. The **Join** button becomes active about **10
   minutes before** the session starts.
2. **Use a supported browser.** We recommend the latest **Google Chrome** or
   **Microsoft Edge**. Disable aggressive ad-blockers for the portal domain.
3. **Check your network.** Live classes need a stable connection. Corporate VPNs and
   firewalls sometimes block streaming — try a different network if possible.
4. **Refresh and re-join.** Reload the page and click **Join** again. If you were
   removed, your session may have dropped briefly — rejoining is safe.
5. **Clear cache** or try an incognito window if the page won't load.

If the **Join** button is missing entirely at class time, the class may have been
rescheduled — check your email and the announcements panel.

## I missed a live class — is there a recording?

Yes. Recordings are posted under **My Learning → Live Classes → Recordings**,
usually **within 24 hours** of the session.

## I can't log in to the platform

- Use **Forgot Password** on the login page to reset your password.
- Make sure you're signing in with the **email you enrolled with**.
- If you signed up with Google/LinkedIn, use that same social login button.
- Still stuck after a reset? Raise a ticket from the Help Center.

## My course isn't showing on my dashboard

This usually means the purchase is linked to a different email. Confirm which email
you used at checkout, and contact support with your order ID so we can merge or
transfer the enrollment.

## Getting more help

For anything not covered here, open the **Help Center**, choose **Platform &
Access**, and raise a ticket. Include screenshots and your browser/OS details to
speed up resolution.
