# Capstone Project — Guidelines & Rubric

The capstone is your opportunity to apply everything you've learned to an
end-to-end problem. This guide explains expectations, milestones, and how your
work is graded.

## Deliverables

1. **Problem statement** — the question you are answering and why it matters.
2. **Dataset & EDA** — source, size, and an exploratory data analysis with at least
   three visualizations.
3. **Modeling** — at least two approaches compared on a shared evaluation metric.
4. **Results & discussion** — what worked, what didn't, and the limitations.
5. **Final report (PDF)** and a **5–7 minute recorded walkthrough**.

## Milestones

- **Week 1:** submit your problem statement and dataset for mentor approval.
- **Week 3:** submit EDA notebook and a baseline model.
- **Week 5:** submit final model, report, and recording.

## Grading rubric (100 points)

- Problem framing & clarity — 15
- Data handling & EDA — 20
- Modeling rigor (baselines, validation, tuning) — 30
- Results interpretation — 20
- Communication (report + recording) — 15

## If you get stuck

It is normal to feel stuck on a capstone — that is part of real data science. A
productive way to get unstuck:

1. **Restate the goal** in one sentence. What exactly are you trying to predict or
   explain?
2. **Shrink the problem.** Get the simplest possible pipeline working end-to-end on a
   small data sample before improving any single step.
3. **Check the data first.** Most "model" problems are actually data problems —
   missing values, leakage, or mislabeled targets.
4. **Compare to a baseline.** If a simple model already does well, focus your effort
   on the parts that move the metric.
5. **Ask a specific question** in office hours: share what you tried, what you
   expected, and what actually happened.

Mentors are available in weekly office hours and via the discussion forum. Bring a
specific, reproducible question and you'll get much faster help.
